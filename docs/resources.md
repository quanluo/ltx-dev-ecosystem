## Selection and removal rules

A resource remains listed when it has a clear owner, useful documentation,
relevant source or releases, transparent licensing, and an identifiable LTX
use case. We label or remove resources that become unavailable, misleading,
malicious, abandoned without archival context, or primarily designed for link
manipulation.

To suggest a correction, open an issue with the canonical URL, relationship
disclosure, supported version, license, and evidence for the proposed change.
